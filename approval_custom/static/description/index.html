Professional Approval Management

Empower your Odoo Community Edition with enterprise-grade multi-level approval workflows, dynamic routing rules, and seamless user interaction.

Odoo 18.0 Community & Enterprise Ready

Overview

Managing approvals efficiently is vital for maintaining compliance, budget control, and organizational transparency. The Professional Approval Management module brings Enterprise-level workflow control to your Odoo Community system.

Configure multi-stage approvals, route requests based on dynamic condition rules (such as requested amounts, departments, or categories), and allow users to delegate their approval power when on leave.

Key Capabilities

Multi-Level Workflows

Support both sequential and parallel approval steps. Configure multiple validation tiers before a request is officially approved.

Dynamic Rules Engine

Route approvals based on custom criteria such as total amount, department, product category, or requesting employee.

Approval Delegation

Temporarily delegate approval authority to backup colleagues during vacations or leaves, with automatic start and end date controls.

Product Walkthrough

Step 1

Approval Dashboard

Access all your approval categories in one clear, centralized view. Create requests for General Approvals, Purchase Requests, Expense Reports, Business Trips, or Equipment Requests. The dashboard displays the number of pending items awaiting review.

Step 2

Detailed Request Form

Submit comprehensive approval forms complete with request owner, category, department, priority levels, deadlines, and item lines. Attach files directly, outline products or items, and view real-time status flows from submission to validation.

Step 3

Kanban Board Tracking

Track your requests dynamically as they move across stages: To Submit, Submitted, Approved, Refused, and Cancelled. Simple drag-and-drop mechanics or status transitions keep everyone on the same page.

Step 4

Approvals to Review

Approvers can access a dedicated view of pending approvals, listing the request subject, owner, department, activities, and urgency. Quick action buttons speed up the validation or refusal process.
